## 🎯 About The Project

**Veyra AI** was created to eliminate the chaos, hidden fees, and hours wasted on manual expense reviews. 

In modern businesses, invoices arrive scattered across emails, PDFs, and paper receipts. Overlooked duplicate bills, mathematical miscalculations, and incorrect tax rates quietly drain corporate budgets. Veyra AI transforms this workflow into an automated, error-free process by combining multimodal visual intelligence with rigorous financial audit rules.

From the first moment an invoice is uploaded, Veyra AI reads the document, inspects every line item, runs multi-layered verification checks, and presents actionable financial insights—allowing finance teams and business owners to approve payments with absolute confidence.

## 🚀 What The Project Delivers

### 1. 📄 Automated Document Extraction
Drop any invoice or receipt into the system. Veyra AI extracts:
- Vendor identity, billing address, and category
- Invoice number, issue date, and payment due date
- Itemized line items, unit quantities, and individual prices
- Subtotals, applicable tax percentages, tax amounts, and final balances
- Overall document extraction confidence scoring

### 2. 🛡️ 4-Layer Automated Audit Engine
Every ingested invoice passes through an uncompromising verification engine:
- **Duplicate Interception:** Detects identical invoice numbers or duplicate vendor payments submitted across billing cycles.
- **Mathematical Reconciliation:** Verifies that individual line items add up precisely to the stated subtotal, catching silent rounding or calculation errors.
- **Tax Consistency Verification:** Cross-checks billing tax percentages against actual calculated amounts.
- **High-Value Thresholds:** Automatically flags expenses exceeding corporate policy limits (e.g. over $10,000) for dual executive sign-off.

### 3. 👥 Human-in-the-Loop Approval Workflow
Automation assists, but controllers stay in control:
- **Approval Ledger:** Search, filter, and sort invoices by status (*All, Flagged, Pending, Approved, Rejected*) or vendor category.
- **Inspection Modal:** Deep-dive into any invoice to review line items, examine triggered warning flags, and inspect the chronological audit history trail.
- **Actionable Controls:** Approve disbursements with a single click, reject fraudulent or mistaken bills with recorded reasons, or flag items with notes for team investigation.

### 4. 🤖 Grounded AI Financial Assistant
An embedded conversational partner built directly into the ledger:
- Ask questions like: *"Which invoices have errors?"*, *"What was our total cloud spend this month?"*, or *"Show me all pending payments for Northwind Freight."*
- Receive clear, data-backed summaries grounded directly in the active financial ledger.
- One-click query chips to quickly audit flags, spending categories, or quarterly disbursements.

### 5. 📊 Real-Time Spend Intelligence & Analytics
Clear executive visibility into corporate cash outflow:
- **Total Audited Volume:** Real-time counter of total business volume processed.
- **Anomaly Alerts:** Live counter of flagged issues requiring controller intervention.
- **Approved Disbursements:** Total funds cleared for accounts payable.
- **Category & Vendor Distributions:** Visual breakdowns of spending across Cloud Infrastructure, Logistics, SaaS Subscriptions, Legal, and Consulting.

### 6. 🔐 Secure Accounts & User Onboarding
- **Self-Serve Registration:** Sign up or sign in using Google, GitHub, or email through Clerk.
- **Personalized Experience:** Displays your name, profile, and user controls in the header, attaching real reviewer signatures to invoice approvals.
- **Quick Preview Mode:** Instant access capability for guests and evaluators who want to explore the dashboard immediately.

### 7. 🌐 Complete Brand Experience
- **Interactive Landing Showcase:** Hero invoice simulation card, feature cards with animated shader canvas backgrounds, and customer testimonials.
- **Early-Access Waitlist:** Fully interactive signup workflow with live queue position feedback (#412+).
- **Resource Center:** Comprehensive financial guides, articles, and version changelog.
